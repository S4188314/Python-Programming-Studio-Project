Our Mission

VaxTrack helps students, researchers, and public-health decision makers examine preventable infectious diseases with clear, respectful, and evidence-based data views.

The site connects vaccination coverage, infection burden, economic context, and global averages so visitors can move from a broad overview into deeper analysis without needing specialist tools.

How to use this page

Use this page to understand the purpose of the site, the people it supports, and the team responsible for the academic project.
